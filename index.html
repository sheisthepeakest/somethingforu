01 — A letter for you

My Miyu,

There is no occasion today. No birthday, no anniversary no reason for me to be writing you this letter.. Then I thought about it. I realized something every ordinary day I get to have you in my life already feels special to me.

Sometimes I wish I could put everything I feel for you into words. No matter how much I write it never feels like enough. There are many little things I want you to know so many feelings I wish I could explain perfectly but somehow the words always fall a little short of whats in my heart.

You make ordinary moments mean more than they used to. A conversation, a random message from you a little moment we share. Things that might seem small to anyone else can become the kind of things I carry with me throughout my day.. I don't think you always realize just how much those little things mean to me.

I want you to know that you don't have to do anything to be special to me. You don't have to be happy all the time you don't have to have everything figured out. You don't have to pretend that every day is perfect. I want you on the days the quiet days, the days when you don't feel like talking much and the days when you have a thousand things on your mind. I want to keep learning about you understanding you and loving the person you're through all of it.

Miyu if theres one thing I wish I could make you understand it's that you don't need a special day to deserve a little extra love. You don't need a reason to be reminded that you matter. Because for me it's not the big moments that make having you in my life beautiful. It's the ones too. Its knowing that somewhere in this world there's you. Someone who has become such a meaningful part of my life.

Maybe that's why I wanted to make this website for you. Not because something special happened today. Because I was thinking about you as I so often do. I wanted you to have something that reminds you of how I feel. Something you can come back to on any day whenever you want and remember that you are loved.

No today isn't a special occasion.. It's another day I get to love you another day I'm grateful you're part of my life and another ordinary day that means a little more because of you. Every day with you is special, to me, Miyu. Not because every day is perfect. Because you're someone I never want to take for granted. And if I could give you one thing it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes for a moment so you could understand why I feel so lucky to have you in my life.

Yours, always trying to find the right words,
Sam ♡
